The approach

Three kinds of stories.

01

Celebrate milestones

Graduations and achievements give the College's community, from families to alumni, something to cheer for and share.

02

Show real student life

Behind-the-scenes moments in simulation labs and classrooms show prospective students what nursing school actually looks like.

03

Drive deadlines

Countdown campaigns turn program deadlines into timely, faculty-led content that prompts action.
