The challenge

Interest isn't enrollment.

Finishing a free course shows interest, but it doesn't guarantee a purchase. The College needed to follow up with every learner at the right moment, without manual work and without pressuring people who weren't ready to commit.

The strategy

Four decisions that shaped it.

01

Right message, right moment

The journey starts the moment a learner finishes the free course, while interest is highest. Follow-ups are spaced 3 days, 4 days, and 1 week apart to stay present without overwhelming.

02

Smart exits

Before every send, the journey checks the learner's status. Anyone who has already purchased leaves automatically, so no one gets a sales email for something they own.

03

Two paths, one goal

Every email offers the best-value full program and a lower-commitment option: the next single course. Hesitant learners keep moving forward instead of dropping off.

04

Learn from non-buyers

A survey email gathers feedback early, and learners who don't enroll are tagged "Not converted" for future, targeted outreach.
